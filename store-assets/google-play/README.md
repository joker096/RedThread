# Vow — Google Play store assets

This folder contains **real browser captures of the current Vow web prototype**, generated by Playwright. They are for internal review, not a claim that the app is release-ready.

## Phone screenshots

The browser workflow captures the actual app UI at a 540 × 960 CSS viewport with device scale factor 2, producing 1080 × 1920 PNGs (9:16). No device frame or fake interaction is added. The screens cover discovery, compatibility, safety, and the chats preview. The chats screen explicitly says sample conversations are local prototype data; do not present them as live users/messages.

Files:
- `phone/01-discover.png`
- `phone/02-compatibility.png`
- `phone/03-safety.png`
- `phone/04-chats-preview.png`

## Google Play checklist

- [ ] Confirm every screenshot matches the final Android release, not just the web prototype.
- [ ] Replace or remove any preview-only screens before publishing.
- [ ] Check localized store listings and capture localized screenshots where needed.
- [ ] Supply the 512 × 512 app icon and 1024 × 500 feature graphic.
- [ ] Confirm current Play Console rules before upload.

Google Play permits JPEG or 24-bit PNG screenshots without alpha, with each side between 320 and 3840 px and the long side no more than twice the short side. For promotional eligibility, Google recommends at least four screenshots at 1080 px or higher. See the [official preview assets guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en-en).

These files are not a declaration of Play Store compliance or product readiness. The app remains a prototype until release-blocking flows and real-device checks pass.
